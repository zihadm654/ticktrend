# 05: Storefront listing, filters, cards and product page

**What to build:** A shopper can browse and filter watches, compare cards and open a product page that describes a watch. The filter sidebar and sheet use watch groups in watch order. Cards show movement, case size and option count, with the qualifying option's price and stock. The product page replaces the scent pyramid with grouped feature chips and a watch specification table, and the buy box calls variants Options, showing movement, style and condition chips. Search suggestions use watch labels.
